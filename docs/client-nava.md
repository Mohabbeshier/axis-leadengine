Conventions kept from their own cards, not invented:
- Two-figure entries are ranges (their `من` / `الى` columns), rendered low–high.
- Extensions (fit, fix, removal) print `575` against "تحدد من قبل الاختصاصية" —
  shown as "from 575" with "تُحدَّد مع الاختصاصية" beneath, never as a flat price.
- The nail card's footnote (tools, 35 ر.س) and the lash refill condition (50%
  remaining after two weeks) ride with their own chapters.
- Their card prints `بارافين يد 150 / بارافين قدم 150` against the English
  "Paraffin Feet / Paraffin Hands" — the two are swapped on their card. Page
  uses بارافين لليدين / بارافين للقدمين. **Worth confirming with them.**
- Fresha still lists no priced menu; this came from the client directly.

## Positioning read (mine, to be confirmed)

93k followers with an active Snapchat and TikTok says the demand engine is
already social; the missing piece is a destination that converts that
audience without a DM queue. The interior evidence — floor-to-ceiling glass
over the city, black marble, curved boucle seating, orchids — supports a
genuinely premium register rather than an aspirational one. Tenth floor with
a skyline view is a real differentiator almost no Jeddah salon can claim,
and it should carry the opening scene.
